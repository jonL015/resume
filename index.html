Flood Risk Assistant for Ontario Farmers

Research Assistant, HydroClimEX Lab, Western University · May 2026 – Present

  • Building an LLM-powered assistant that estimates flood risk in Ontario and explains it to farmers in plain, tailored terms.
  • The assistant interviews each farmer about location, farm type and concerns, then produces a customized analysis.
  • Grounds answers with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over research papers, stream-gauge records, flood maps and watershed data to reduce hallucination.
  • Adapted from WildfireGPT (Xie et al., 2025) and applied to Canadian flood risk.

Chaos in a Bouncing Polygonal Die

Research Assistant, Rock Mechanics Lab, Western University · May – Oct 2025

  • Simulated a bouncing regular polygonal die in MATLAB and mapped settling time and maximum horizontal extent across drop height, initial orientation and polygon order.
  • Ran a timestep convergence test to confirm the trends are reproducible.
  • Concluded the dynamics are complicated rather than strictly chaotic. First-author paper submitted to AIP Advances.
